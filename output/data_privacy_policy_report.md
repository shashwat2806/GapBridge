## Recommendations

### GOVERN (12.9% - needs attention)

1. Develop a clear organizational mission statement that encompasses cybersecurity objectives (GV.OC-01), and ensure it is communicated to all stakeholders for understanding and buy-in.

2. Conduct regular meetings with internal and external stakeholders to discuss their needs, expectations, and concerns regarding cybersecurity risk management (GV.OC-02). This will help in aligning cybersecurity efforts with the organization's goals and stakeholder requirements.

3. Establish risk management objectives that are agreed upon by organizational stakeholders (GV.RM-01), and define risk appetite and tolerance statements to guide decision making (GV.RM-02). These steps will foster a more informed approach to managing cybersecurity risks within the organization.

### IDENTIFY (14.3% - needs attention)

1. Implement an asset inventory management system that includes hardware, software, services, and systems (ID.AM-1, ID.AM-2) to maintain a comprehensive list of all organization assets.

2. Establish a vulnerability scanning and management program following best practices, such as NVD or CVE databases, to regularly identify, validate, and record vulnerabilities in assets (ID.RA-01).

3. Subscribe to cyber threat intelligence feeds from trusted sources like ISACs, DHS US-CERT, or other information sharing forums to stay informed of potential threats and improve organizational risk management (ID.RA-02).

### PROTECT (27.3% - needs attention)

1. Implement a centralized identity and access management system to ensure all users, services, and hardware are managed effectively, following PR.AA-01 and PR.PS-01 controls.

2. Provide regular cybersecurity awareness training for individuals in specialized roles to enhance their understanding of security risks and improve practices, as outlined by PR.AT-02.

3. Install access control systems and surveillance cameras at critical infrastructure locations to monitor physical access to assets commensurate with risk, referencing PR.AA-06 and PR.PS-01 controls.

### DETECT (18.2% - needs attention)

1. Implement an incident response platform to automate the correlation of information from multiple sources (DE.AE-03) and analyze potentially adverse events for a better understanding (DE.AE-02).

2. Deploy network monitoring tools to regularly scan for suspicious activities that could indicate potential adverse events (DE.CM-01) and integrate cyber threat intelligence into the analysis (DE.AE-07).

3. Establish clear incident criteria and implement a system for ongoing monitoring of the physical environment and external service providers' activities to ensure prompt declaration of incidents when they occur (DE.CM-02 and DE.AE-08).

### RESPOND (7.7% - needs attention)

1. Develop and document a clear incident response plan that includes procedures for coordinating with relevant third parties (RS.MA-01), and establish guidelines for triaging, validating, categorizing, prioritizing, and escalating incidents (RS.MA-02, RS.MA-03, RS.MA-04).

2. Implement a system for initiating incident recovery based on predefined criteria (RS.MA-05) and establish protocols for notifying internal and external stakeholders in a timely manner (RS.CO-02, RS.CO-03).

3. Establish a post-incident analysis process to determine the root cause of incidents and improve incident response capabilities moving forward (RS.AN-03).

### RECOVER (0.0% - needs attention)

1. Develop and regularly update an incident response plan that includes clear steps for executing recovery actions as outlined in RC.RP-01 and RC.RP-02.

2. Implement a backup verification process before using them for restoration, as specified in RC.RP-03, to ensure the integrity of critical assets during recovery.

3. Establish clear communication protocols for updating internal and external stakeholders on the progress of incident recovery, following guidelines provided in RC.CO-03 and RC.CO-04.
