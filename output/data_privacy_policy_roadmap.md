# GapBridge Improvement Roadmap

## Short Term (0-3 months)

- **RC.RP-01** [RECOVER, function at 0.0% coverage] The recovery portion of the incident response plan is executed once initiated from the incident response process
- **RC.RP-02** [RECOVER, function at 0.0% coverage] Recovery actions are selected, scoped, prioritized, and performed
- **RC.RP-03** [RECOVER, function at 0.0% coverage] The integrity of backups and other restoration assets is verified before using them for restoration
- **RC.RP-04** [RECOVER, function at 0.0% coverage] Critical mission functions and cybersecurity risk management are considered to establish post-incident operational norms
- **RC.RP-05** [RECOVER, function at 0.0% coverage] The integrity of restored assets is verified, systems and services are restored, and normal operating status is confirmed
- **RC.RP-06** [RECOVER, function at 0.0% coverage] The end of incident recovery is declared based on criteria, and incident-related documentation is completed
- **RC.CO-03** [RECOVER, function at 0.0% coverage] Recovery activities and progress in restoring operational capabilities are communicated to designated internal and external stakeholders
- **RC.CO-04** [RECOVER, function at 0.0% coverage] Public updates on incident recovery are shared using approved methods and messaging
- **RS.MA-01** [RESPOND, function at 7.7% coverage] The incident response plan is executed in coordination with relevant third parties once an incident is declared
- **RS.MA-02** [RESPOND, function at 7.7% coverage] Incident reports are triaged and validated
- **RS.MA-03** [RESPOND, function at 7.7% coverage] Incidents are categorized and prioritized
- **RS.MA-04** [RESPOND, function at 7.7% coverage] Incidents are escalated or elevated as needed
- **RS.MA-05** [RESPOND, function at 7.7% coverage] The criteria for initiating incident recovery are applied
- **RS.CO-02** [RESPOND, function at 7.7% coverage] Internal and external stakeholders are notified of incidents
- **RS.CO-03** [RESPOND, function at 7.7% coverage] Information is shared with designated internal and external stakeholders, consistent with response plans
- **RS.AN-03** [RESPOND, function at 7.7% coverage] Analysis is performed to establish what has taken place during an incident and the root cause of the incident
- **RS.AN-06** [RESPOND, function at 7.7% coverage] Actions performed during an investigation are recorded, and the records' integrity and provenance are preserved
- **RS.AN-08** [RESPOND, function at 7.7% coverage] An incident's magnitude is estimated and validated
- **RS.MI-01** [RESPOND, function at 7.7% coverage] Incidents are contained
- **RS.MI-02** [RESPOND, function at 7.7% coverage] Incidents are eradicated
