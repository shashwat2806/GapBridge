# GapBridge Improvement Roadmap

## Short Term (0-3 months)

- **RS.MA-01** [RESPOND, function at 0.0% coverage] The incident response plan is executed in coordination with relevant third parties once an incident is declared
- **RS.MA-02** [RESPOND, function at 0.0% coverage] Incident reports are triaged and validated
- **RS.MA-03** [RESPOND, function at 0.0% coverage] Incidents are categorized and prioritized
- **RS.MA-04** [RESPOND, function at 0.0% coverage] Incidents are escalated or elevated as needed
- **RS.MA-05** [RESPOND, function at 0.0% coverage] The criteria for initiating incident recovery are applied
- **RS.CO-02** [RESPOND, function at 0.0% coverage] Internal and external stakeholders are notified of incidents
- **RS.CO-03** [RESPOND, function at 0.0% coverage] Information is shared with designated internal and external stakeholders, consistent with response plans
- **RS.AN-03** [RESPOND, function at 0.0% coverage] Analysis is performed to establish what has taken place during an incident and the root cause of the incident
- **RS.AN-06** [RESPOND, function at 0.0% coverage] Actions performed during an investigation are recorded, and the records' integrity and provenance are preserved
- **RS.AN-07** [RESPOND, function at 0.0% coverage] Incident data and metadata are collected, and their integrity and provenance are preserved
- **RS.AN-08** [RESPOND, function at 0.0% coverage] An incident's magnitude is estimated and validated
- **RS.MI-01** [RESPOND, function at 0.0% coverage] Incidents are contained
- **RS.MI-02** [RESPOND, function at 0.0% coverage] Incidents are eradicated
- **DE.AE-02** [DETECT, function at 9.1% coverage] Potentially adverse events are analyzed to better understand associated activities
- **DE.AE-03** [DETECT, function at 9.1% coverage] Information is correlated from multiple sources
- **DE.AE-06** [DETECT, function at 9.1% coverage] Information on adverse events is provided to authorized staff and tools
- **DE.AE-07** [DETECT, function at 9.1% coverage] Cyber threat intelligence and other contextual information are integrated into the analysis
- **DE.AE-08** [DETECT, function at 9.1% coverage] Incidents are declared when adverse events meet the defined incident criteria
- **DE.CM-01** [DETECT, function at 9.1% coverage] Networks and network services are monitored to find potentially adverse events
- **DE.CM-02** [DETECT, function at 9.1% coverage] The physical environment is monitored to find potentially adverse events
- **DE.CM-03** [DETECT, function at 9.1% coverage] Personnel activity and technology usage are monitored to find potentially adverse events
- **DE.CM-06** [DETECT, function at 9.1% coverage] External service provider activities and services are monitored to find potentially adverse events
- **DE.CM-09** [DETECT, function at 9.1% coverage] Computing hardware and software, runtime environments, and their data are monitored to find potentially adverse events

## Mid Term (3-6 months)

- **RC.RP-01** [RECOVER, function at 12.5% coverage] The recovery portion of the incident response plan is executed once initiated from the incident response process
- **RC.RP-02** [RECOVER, function at 12.5% coverage] Recovery actions are selected, scoped, prioritized, and performed
- **RC.RP-03** [RECOVER, function at 12.5% coverage] The integrity of backups and other restoration assets is verified before using them for restoration
- **RC.RP-05** [RECOVER, function at 12.5% coverage] The integrity of restored assets is verified, systems and services are restored, and normal operating status is confirmed
- **RC.RP-06** [RECOVER, function at 12.5% coverage] The end of incident recovery is declared based on criteria, and incident-related documentation is completed
- **RC.CO-03** [RECOVER, function at 12.5% coverage] Recovery activities and progress in restoring operational capabilities are communicated to designated internal and external stakeholders
- **RC.CO-04** [RECOVER, function at 12.5% coverage] Public updates on incident recovery are shared using approved methods and messaging
- **PR.AA-01** [PROTECT, function at 13.6% coverage] Identities and credentials for authorized users, services, and hardware are managed by the organization
- **PR.AA-02** [PROTECT, function at 13.6% coverage] Identities are proofed and bound to credentials based on the context of interactions
- **PR.AA-03** [PROTECT, function at 13.6% coverage] Users, services, and hardware are authenticated
- **PR.AA-04** [PROTECT, function at 13.6% coverage] Identity assertions are protected, conveyed, and verified
- **PR.AA-05** [PROTECT, function at 13.6% coverage] Access permissions, entitlements, and authorizations are defined in a policy, managed, enforced, and reviewed, and incorporate the principles of least privilege and separation of duties
- **PR.AT-02** [PROTECT, function at 13.6% coverage] Individuals in specialized roles are provided with awareness and training so that they possess the knowledge and skills to perform relevant tasks with cybersecurity risks in mind
- **PR.DS-01** [PROTECT, function at 13.6% coverage] The confidentiality, integrity, and availability of data-at-rest are protected
- **PR.DS-02** [PROTECT, function at 13.6% coverage] The confidentiality, integrity, and availability of data-in-transit are protected
- **PR.DS-10** [PROTECT, function at 13.6% coverage] The confidentiality, integrity, and availability of data-in-use are protected
- **PR.DS-11** [PROTECT, function at 13.6% coverage] The confidentiality, integrity, and availability of data-in-use are protected
- **PR.PS-01** [PROTECT, function at 13.6% coverage] Configuration management practices are established and applied
- **PR.PS-02** [PROTECT, function at 13.6% coverage] Software is maintained, replaced, and removed commensurate with risk
- **PR.PS-03** [PROTECT, function at 13.6% coverage] Hardware is maintained, replaced, and removed commensurate with risk
- **PR.PS-04** [PROTECT, function at 13.6% coverage] Log records are generated and made available for continuous monitoring
- **PR.PS-05** [PROTECT, function at 13.6% coverage] Installation and execution of unauthorized software are prevented
- **PR.PS-06** [PROTECT, function at 13.6% coverage] Secure software development practices are integrated, and their performance is monitored throughout the software development life cycle
- **PR.IR-01** [PROTECT, function at 13.6% coverage] Networks and environments are protected from unauthorized logical access and usage
- **PR.IR-03** [PROTECT, function at 13.6% coverage] Mechanisms are implemented to achieve resilience requirements in normal and adverse situations
- **PR.IR-04** [PROTECT, function at 13.6% coverage] Adequate resource capacity to ensure availability is maintained
