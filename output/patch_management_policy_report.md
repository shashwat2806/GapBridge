## Recommendations

### GOVERN (3.2% - needs attention)

1. Establish and document a clear organizational mission statement that defines cybersecurity priorities, aligning with GV.OC-01.
2. Conduct regular meetings with internal and external stakeholders to understand their needs, expectations, and concerns regarding cybersecurity, in line with GV.OC-02.
3. Develop a comprehensive set of risk management objectives and communicate them to all stakeholders, including a documented risk appetite and tolerance, referencing GV.RM-01 and GV.RM-02.

### IDENTIFY (0.0% - needs attention)

1. Implement an asset management system to maintain inventories of hardware (ID.AM-1), software (ID.AM-2), and services (ID.AM-4) used within the organization, including up-to-date information on their locations, versions, and associated risks.

2. Document authorized network communication and internal/external data flows (ID.AM-3), creating a visual representation to aid in understanding the organization's network topology and identifying potential vulnerabilities or anomalies.

3. Establish a regular vulnerability assessment program to identify, validate, and record vulnerabilities in assets (ID.RA-01) as part of a comprehensive asset management strategy for their secure lifecycle management (ID.AM-8).

### PROTECT (4.5% - needs attention)

1. Implement a centralized identity and access management system to address PR.AA-01 and PR.AA-05, ensuring identities, credentials, access permissions, entitlements, and authorizations are managed effectively with least privilege principles.

2. To tackle PR.AA-02 and PR.AT-01, establish a regular cybersecurity awareness training program for all employees to educate them about the importance of secure authentication practices in their day-to-day activities.

3. Monitor and enforce physical access control measures (PR.AA-06) by installing CCTV cameras, utilizing smart card readers or biometric systems at sensitive areas, and conducting regular audits to ensure compliance with established security policies.

### DETECT (0.0% - needs attention)

1. Implement a Security Information and Event Management (SIEM) system to automate correlation of data from multiple sources (DE.AE-03, DE.CM-01), enhancing our ability to detect potentially adverse events.

2. Establish an Incident Response Team trained to analyze and understand the impact and scope of adverse events (DE.AE-04) as part of the incident declaration process (DE.AE-08).

3. Integrate cyber threat intelligence feeds into existing monitoring systems (DE.AE-07) to improve our capability in detecting and responding to potential security incidents.

### RESPOND (0.0% - needs attention)

1. Develop a clear and comprehensive incident response plan that includes coordination procedures with relevant third parties (RS.MA-01). This plan should outline how to execute the incident response in collaboration with involved parties once an incident is declared.

2. Establish an efficient triage and validation process for incident reports (RS.MA-02). This process should involve assessing the urgency, scope, and potential impact of each report to ensure prompt and appropriate action is taken.

3. Implement a system for categorizing and prioritizing incidents based on their severity and potential impact (RS.MA-03). This system can help ensure that critical incidents are addressed first and that resources are allocated effectively throughout the incident response process.

### RECOVER (0.0% - needs attention)

1. Develop and formalize an incident response plan that explicitly outlines steps for execution (RC.RP-01) and selection, scoping, prioritization, and performance of recovery actions (RC.RP-02).

2. Implement regular backups and establish a verification process to ensure the integrity of these backups before using them for restoration (RC.RP-03).

3. Establish clear criteria for declaring the end of incident recovery and create a system for documenting incident-related information (RC.RP-06). Additionally, implement communication strategies to keep internal and external stakeholders informed about recovery activities and progress (RC.CO-03).
